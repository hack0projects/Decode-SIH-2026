# Class 10 English — Chapter Summaries

**Subject:** English
**NCERT Book:** First Flight (Prose & Poetry), Footprints without Feet (Supplementary Reader)
**Topic Tag:** english-summary

## FIRST FLIGHT — Prose Chapters

### 1. A Letter to God (G.L. Fuentes)
**Summary:** A poor farmer named Lencho, whose crops are destroyed by hail, writes a letter to God asking for money. Postal workers, moved by his faith, collect money for him — but it's less than he asked for. Lencho, believing God's postal workers must have stolen the rest, writes another angry letter accusing "God's employees" of theft, showing his unwavering, almost naive faith.

**Key theme:** Faith, and the irony of human nature even in receiving charity.

### 2. Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom
**Summary:** An excerpt from Mandela's autobiography describing his presidential inauguration and reflecting on the long struggle against apartheid, freedom's true meaning, and the courage of those who fought oppression.

**Key theme:** Freedom, courage, and the fight against injustice.

### 3. Two Stories about Flying — I. His First Flight (Liam O'Flaherty)
**Summary:** A young seagull is afraid to fly, encouraged eventually by hunger and his family, learning through fear-facing that flying is far easier than he imagined once he tries.

**Key theme:** Overcoming fear through necessity and courage.

### 3. Two Stories about Flying — II. Black Aeroplane
**Summary:** A pilot flying through a dangerous storm is guided to safety by a mysterious black aeroplane that disappears afterward, implied to be a ghostly or supernatural guide.

**Key theme:** Mystery, guidance in crisis.

### 4. From the Diary of Anne Frank
**Summary:** An excerpt from the diary of a young Jewish girl hiding from the Nazis during World War II, describing her personality, relationships with family members in hiding, and her introspective thoughts as a teenager.

**Key theme:** Resilience, identity, and the human spirit amid crisis.

### 5. The Hundred Dresses – I & II (Eleanor Estes)
**Summary:** A poor girl, Wanda, is teased by classmates for claiming to own a hundred dresses. After she moves away, her classmates discover she really did draw a hundred beautiful dress designs, and they feel deep guilt for their unkindness.

**Key theme:** Bullying, regret, and empathy.

### 6. Glimpses of India — I. A Baker from Goa
**Summary:** Describes the tradition and cultural significance of bread bakers in Goa, and how this profession has been part of Goan life for generations.

### 6. Glimpses of India — II. Coorg
**Summary:** A travel description of Coorg (Karnataka), covering its scenic beauty, culture, and unique history.

### 6. Glimpses of India — III. Tea from Assam
**Summary:** Explores the origin and cultivation process of tea in Assam through a conversation on a train journey.

### 7. Mijbil the Otter (Gavin Maxwell)
**Summary:** The author's account of adopting and bonding with a pet otter, exploring themes of human-animal companionship.

### 8. Madam Rides the Bus
**Summary:** A young girl's first solo bus journey, exploring themes of independence, courage, and self-discovery.

### 9. The Sermon at Benares
**Summary:** Describes the story of Buddha's teaching to Kisa Gotami, who is grieving her dead child, using the parable of the mustard seed to teach her about the universality of death and suffering.

**Key theme:** Buddhist philosophy, acceptance of mortality.

### 10. The Proposal (Anton Chekhov) — a comic play
**Summary:** A humorous play about a nervous man proposing marriage, where the couple argues over trivial matters (land ownership, hunting dogs) instead of focusing on the actual proposal, satirizing petty disputes.

## FIRST FLIGHT — Poetry Chapters

### Dust of Snow (Robert Frost)
**Summary:** A short poem about how a small, simple moment (snow falling from a tree) changes the poet's mood on an otherwise bad day.

### Fire and Ice (Robert Frost)
**Summary:** A metaphorical poem about the destructive nature of desire (fire) and hatred (ice), reflecting on how the world might end.

### A Tiger in the Zoo
**Summary:** Contrasts the confined life of a caged tiger with how majestic and free it would be in its natural habitat.

### How to Tell Wild Animals
**Summary:** A humorous poem describing (often absurdly) how to identify different wild animals.

### The Ball Poem (John Berryman)
**Summary:** Reflects on a boy losing his ball and the deeper lesson about loss, growing up, and learning to cope with life's disappointments.

### Amanda! (Robin Klein)
**Summary:** A poem about a girl who imagines being various free, fantastical characters to escape the constant nagging and restrictions imposed by adults.

## FOOTPRINTS WITHOUT FEET — Supplementary Reader

### 1. A Triumph of Surgery
**Summary:** A humorous vet story about an overfed, overweight dog whose owner finally listens to the vet's advice on diet and exercise.

### 2. The Thief's Story
**Summary:** A young thief develops an unexpected bond of trust with the man he plans to rob, ultimately choosing honesty over theft.

### 3. The Midnight Visitor
**Summary:** A clever secret agent story where the protagonist outwits a spy through a fabricated dramatic story, tricking him into revealing himself.

### 4. A Question of Trust
**Summary:** A story of two con artists trying to outsmart each other, exploring irony and the theme of trust and deception.

### 5. Footprints without Feet
**Summary:** A science fiction story about a scientist who invents an invisibility formula and misuses it for criminal purposes, exploring themes of power and morality.

### 6. The Making of a Scientist
**Summary:** Describes the childhood curiosity and passion for science of Richard Ebright, who became a renowned scientist, highlighting how early curiosity develops into expertise.

### 7. The Necklace (Guy de Maupassant)
**Summary:** A woman borrows a necklace for a party, loses it, and spends years working to repay its replacement cost — only to later discover the original was fake and worthless, a classic tale of irony and vanity.

### 8. Bholi
**Summary:** A story about a girl who overcomes her lack of confidence (due to a childhood illness and social stigma) through education and self-respect, ultimately standing up against an unjust marriage arrangement.

### 9. The Book That Saved the Earth
**Summary:** A comic science-fiction play about Martians who misunderstand human literature (like "Mother Goose" nursery rhymes), leading to a humorous failed invasion of Earth.

## Practice Questions (sample format for exam prep)

1. What lesson does "The Hundred Dresses" teach about bullying and regret?
2. How does "The Necklace" use irony to make its point about vanity and pride?
3. Compare the themes of freedom in "A Tiger in the Zoo" and "Amanda!"
4. What does "The Sermon at Benares" teach about grief and mortality?
